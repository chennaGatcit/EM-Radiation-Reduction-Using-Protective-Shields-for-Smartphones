# EM-Radiation-Reduction-Using-Protective-Shields-for-Smartphones
Our project “EM Radiation Reduction Using Protective Shields for Smartphones” presents an FSS-based design to minimize harmful EM radiation without affecting normal signals. Simulated using ANSYS HFSS, it achieved dual-band attenuation at 2.86 GHz and 6.27 GHz. 

Abstract:
Smartphones have become inseparable from everyday
life, leading to continuous exposure of users to
electromagnetic (EM) radiation generated by their
antennas. Long-term interaction with radio-frequency
(RF) energy has raised concerns about possible biological
and thermal effects. This work introduces a compact
Frequency Selective Surface (FSS)–based shield that can
be embedded inside a smartphone cover to reduce
unwanted EM radiation without disturbing normal
communication. The proposed structure was designed
and analyzed in ANSYS HFSS, targeting sub-6 GHz 5G
operating bands. The study examined reflection
characteristics (S₁₁), resonant frequencies, and
bandwidths for various array configurations. Simulation
results indicate that the FSS achieves effective dual-band
attenuation around 2.86 GHz and 6.27 GHz, offering
notable radiation reduction while remaining lightweight
and manufacturable.
Keywords — Frequency Selective Surface (FSS),
Electromagnetic Radiation, SAR Reduction, HFSS
Simulation, Sub-6 GHz Band, Smartphone Shielding.
